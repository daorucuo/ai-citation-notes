How an issue works

Not a news digest. 3–5 items, each written on its own, never paired.

Each item has three beats: what happened ／ what it means ／ so what to watch. If one beat is missing, it does not run.

Every issue also carries what we could not figure out — an item we saw but cannot judge. It stays there.
